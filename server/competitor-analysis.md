# Competitor Analysis Report

## Company Context
App studio building portfolio of apps marketed organically using TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram.

## Executive Summary
The organic social media marketing space for apps is highly competitive with agencies charging between $2,000-$20,000/month depending on portfolio and reach. Success stories show clear ROI with 200%+ growth when using TikTok creator marketing vs traditional paid ads.

#### 1. TikTok Marketing Agencies (2026)
- **Tier 1 Agencies**: Charge $5,000-$20,000/month
- **Mid-Tier**: $2,000-$5,000/month

- **ROI Evidence**: TikTok organic reach now matches Instagram for many apps, with higher engagement (3-5x engagement rates vs Instagram)

#### 3. App Marketing Agencies (Organic Focus)
- **Adworkly**: Specializes in organic growth via influencer partnerships
- **Pricing**: Typically $3,000-$8,000/month for startups

1. **Organic-First Strategy**:
   - Lower CAC than paid ads
   - Higher long-term engagement
   - More sustainable growth

2. **Multi-Platform Portfolio**:
   - TikTok: High engagement, Gen Z reach
   - YouTube: Long-form, search traffic

3. **B2B Focus**:

### Recommendations

1. **Pricing Strategy**:
   - Development: Fixed project pricing ($30K-$100K per app)
   - Marketing: Retainer model ($5K-$15K/month)
   - Success-based: 10-20% revenue share

2. **Growth Hacking**:
   - TikTok creator partnerships: Start with $500-1K per post
   - YouTube tutorials: Build SEO-driven content
   - Cross-platform reposting: Maximize reach

3. **Portfolio Focus**:
   - Start with 3-5 high-quality apps in B2B category
   - Showcase each app's organic growth journey
   - Use metrics (downloads, retention, revenue) as social proof

4. **Outreach**:
   - LinkedIn for B2B leads
   - Twitter/X for thought leadership
   - TikTok for creator network
